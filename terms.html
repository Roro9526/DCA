01Who this covers

DCA Publisher is a personal tool built and run by a single operator. It is not offered to the public, does not accept sign-ups, and these terms exist only to satisfy TikTok's app-registration requirement that every app declare its terms.

02What the tool does

  • Renders investment-simulation ("dollar-cost averaging") videos from market price data.
  • Connects to TikTok's Content Posting API, using the video.upload scope, to send those videos as private drafts to the operator's own TikTok account.
  • Never posts publicly on its own — every video still requires the operator to open TikTok and manually finish and publish it.

03Acceptable use

The tool is used solely by its operator, on the operator's own TikTok account, for content the operator created. It is not used to post on behalf of anyone else, to spam, or to distribute content that violates TikTok's own Community Guidelines — those still apply to whatever gets published from the drafts this tool creates.
